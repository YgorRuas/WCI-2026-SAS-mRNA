# Learn More: Supplementary Data & Extended Rationale

Welcome to the extended data section of our project. To keep the poster concise and visually engaging, we have compiled all the underlying raw data, 3D renderings, and validation metrics here. 

Explore the directories below to dive deeper into our *in silico* methodology:

## 🧬 3D Protein Structures
This directory contains high-resolution renderings of the ATP synthase subunit alpha (SAS) antigen. You will find:
* The native structure.
* The mutated structure (124 T-G and 126 T-G).
* Close-up views of the mutation sites mitigating the O-glycosylation anchors.
* **[📁 Access the 3D Structures Folder](assets/3D_structures/)**

## 📊 Software Data & Validation Reports
Here we provide the quantitative data that validates our structural approach:
* **AlphaFold 3 Reports:** Confirming structural stability with maximum pLDDT scores (100) in the manipulated regions.
* **DiscoTope 3.0 Metrics:** Statistical confirmation that B-cell conformational epitopes remained fully exposed and accommodated the induced microflexibility.
* **[📁 Access the Software Data Folder](assets/software_data/)**
